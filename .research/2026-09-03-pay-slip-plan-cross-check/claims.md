## Overall assessment

The source plan is suitable as a strong design brief, but `final verified` overstates its evidence. The implementation decision is `GO for the approved design/specification phase`, not yet `GO for production payroll`. U01-U05 now have owner-approved product-design answers, so the unresolved product-design blocker count is zero. Actual independent specialist sign-off, provider/domain/device verification, signed golden-case execution, security/runtime testing, backup restore, and two parallel payroll cycles remain explicit not-run release gates.
