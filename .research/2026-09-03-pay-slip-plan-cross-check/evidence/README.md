# Evidence directory

No remote source copies were downloaded. `sources.json` records the official URLs reviewed as of 2026-09-03, and `claims.md` records the resulting claim boundaries.

Before production use, place only non-secret, reviewable evidence here, such as:

- signed rule-matrix approval;
- redacted independent golden cases and expected outputs;
- legal delivery-method opinion or approval;
- privacy/data-flow approval;
- backup/restore receipt;
- two-cycle parallel-run variance reports.

Never store credentials, production payroll exports, identity documents, bank details, or unredacted employee data in this research bundle.
