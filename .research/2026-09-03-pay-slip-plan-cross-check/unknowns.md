## Decisions received 2026-09-04

- Product boundary: one named employer, not a multi-company product.
- Core product boundary: capture attendance/time, calculate payroll from approved time, and produce payslips with itemized deductions and explanations.
- Current operating state: there is no fixed attendance system; this application will become the authoritative attendance source for payroll after review and approval.
- Attendance interaction: primary MVP is employee code + PIN + clock-in/out on the shared iPOS browser tab, followed by owner/accountant exception review and period approval. A rotating-QR personal-phone route is optional and cannot block MVP release.
- Optional phone authentication: allow code + PIN on every use, or a remembered-device session created only after owner approval. Store only a server-side hash of the session token, use a secure browser cookie, show registered devices to the employee/owner, and support immediate individual revocation; do not fingerprint personal phones.
- Attendance proof/privacy: do not collect photos, facial templates, or GPS. Use registered kiosk/session evidence, PIN, single-use rotating QR for phone events, duplicate/impossible-sequence and schedule anomaly flags, immutable raw events, and owner/accountant period approval.
- Outage policy: online-only. Never acknowledge a clock event until the server has durably accepted it. On failure, display a clear not-recorded message; the accountant may enter a later correction with the claimed time and reason, and the owner must approve it before it can affect payroll.
- Staff scheduling: include reusable weekly shift templates plus effective-dated per-day and public-holiday exceptions. Compare schedule with actual attendance and flag missing, late, early, overlapping, out-of-hours, holiday, and excess-time cases. Do not include employee leave requests or shift-swapping workflows in MVP.
- Break treatment: all rest breaks are paid. Employees do not clock break start/end, and the payroll engine never auto-deducts scheduled or assumed break minutes. Planned rest windows may be recorded on the schedule only for operational and compliance review.
- Explicitly excluded from MVP: tax/insurance filing submission, accounting exports, bank-payment files, and automatic money movement.
- Hosting preference: local company-controlled host in Vietnam; exact email and backup data paths remain open.
- Employment population: full-time and part-time workers only, each signing the corresponding 12-month fixed-term labour contract from day one.
- Probation: six days inside the labour contract, with no salary/rate change after probation; it is not a separate under-three-month contract phase.
- Source templates reviewed: `G:\Becky\Employment Contract\(mẫu) Hợp đồng lao động (full-time).docx` and `G:\Becky\Employment Contract\(mẫu) Hợp đồng lao động part-time.docx`.
- Payslip delivery: an authorized app user enters the employee email address and the app sends the employee's payslip by email.
- Delivery format: the payslip PDF is attached directly to the email; the MVP does not require an employee download portal or externally reachable signed links.
- Email sender: the company's current Gmail account.
- Additional requested delivery channel: Zalo, subject to an official Zalo OA/OpenAPI route; personal-account browser/UI automation is not an accepted production design.
- Current Zalo state: personal account only. Desired future state: switch the channel configuration to a company Zalo Official Account without changing payroll or historical payslip records.
- Approved Zalo transition: the personal account is manual handoff only; automated sending through a personal Zalo account is excluded. Automation may be enabled only after a verified company Zalo OA/OpenAPI adapter is configured and tested.
- Access pattern: the authoritative service/database is shared rather than copied independently onto multiple PCs. Owner/accountant remote access and shop attendance now require an explicitly designed secure inter-site route; they cannot rely on one office LAN. There is no current company domain.
- Local host: a dedicated, always-on Windows machine at the owner's home will host the application and authoritative database. Application availability depends on this host, the home Internet connection, and the selected private/tunnel path being healthy.
- Cloudflare route: the owner confirmed the home Windows host can remain on 24/7. For temporary development/pilot access, use the already-installed `cloudflared` binary to create a temporary Cloudflare Tunnel URL only after the attendance endpoint is ready. Cloudflare WARP is installed and running but is not the attendance tunnel. Do not expose the payroll administration UI or database, do not open router ports, and do not treat the detected binary as proof that a tunnel is configured or running. For production, replace the temporary URL with a named Cloudflare Tunnel and an owner-controlled stable domain; exact account/domain setup remains a release task.
- QR boundary: a QR code is an identifier or short-lived challenge, not the network connection and not sufficient proof of presence by itself. It must not contain employee personal data, salary data, a reusable authentication secret, or the home server's address.
- Shop device: the existing Android iPOS device is the confirmed attendance kiosk. iPOS runs as an app, while the attendance URL can remain open in a separate browser tab. No new attendance hardware is assumed, and installing Tailscale, another VPN client, or a custom app on this device is excluded. Still unknown: exact model/browser version, whether Android/browser lifecycle preserves the tab and kiosk credentials reliably, and whether it has a usable camera or attached 2D scanner.
- Backup readiness: use the separate physical Seagate Expansion drive `G:` and reserve `G:\PaySlip-Backups` for encrypted, versioned application/database/document backups. The directory has not been created yet and existing `G:` contents must never be moved, overwritten, scanned into the application, or treated as backup capacity. The portable recovery secret is printed/written, sealed, and stored separately from the PC and `G:`; an optional second copy may be stored in a password manager, but the secret must never appear in source control, logs, the database, or the backup set. Cloud backup is deliberately deferred to a future plug-in adapter. Require an automated schedule, retention policy, low-space/failure alert, integrity check, and successful restore drill before real payroll data; record the residual lack of offsite protection.
- Payslip attachment protection: every employee receives an individual PDF password through a channel separate from the payslip email. The password is not derived from date of birth or identity-card data, is never included in the email/file name/log, is encrypted at rest, and may be reset with an auditable privileged action.
- Gmail sender: the current company sender is a free `@gmail.com` account rather than a Google Workspace mailbox, and 2-Step Verification is not currently enabled. Gmail API OAuth 2.0 is selected. Development uses a fake/test outbox without the real account; 2-Step Verification, OAuth project/consent setup, real-account authorization, send/revoke testing, and encrypted refresh-token storage are required only at the bounded integration/release gate. The main Gmail password and OAuth tokens must never be stored in payroll records or source control.
- Authorized users at launch: two individually identified accounts—the owner and the accountant. Shared login credentials are excluded. The accountant may enter data, calculate payroll, and create drafts; the owner alone may approve, lock, and send finalized payslips. Every privileged action must be attributed to the acting account in the audit log.
- Confirmed workplace: The Kay's Gelato, Shophouse 14, A6-A7 apartment complex, Nguyễn Ái Quốc road, phường Trấn Biên, tỉnh Đồng Nai. Official Đồng Nai material confirms the A6-A7 site changed from former phường Quang Vinh to phường Trấn Biên. Decree 293/2025/NĐ-CP places Trấn Biên in Region I from 1 January 2026: VND 5,310,000/month and VND 25,500/hour. These values must be effective-dated.
- Workplace scope: all employees included in MVP work only at the confirmed Trấn Biên site, so Region I applies to the full initial employee population. Any later worksite addition must trigger a new effective-dated region determination rather than inheriting Region I automatically.
- Payroll cadence and holiday work: payroll is processed once per calendar month. The application must capture actual public-holiday work by employee/date/hours and calculate/display paid-holiday entitlement separately from the overtime premium. For monthly-paid staff, the ordinary 100% holiday entitlement is normally already included in monthly salary and the holiday-work addition is at least 300%; wage-form-specific formulas and night-work rules must remain separate.
- Store opening hours: Monday closed; Tuesday-Friday currently 15:00-21:00, with a possible future change to 11:00-21:00; Saturday-Sunday 11:00-21:00. No planned opening extends into the statutory night period. Opening hours are schedule constraints, not proof of attendance: payroll must use each employee's captured and approved actual hours and flag any 22:00-06:00 entry for unsupported-night-work handling until that module is explicitly added.
- Excess-time approval: retain the exact raw time outside a published shift and flag it. The accountant proposes whether each segment is ordinary payable time, overtime/public-holiday time, or rejected/non-work time; the owner makes the final approval before payroll. Never infer overtime solely from the schedule variance.
- Compensation entry: choosing `full_time` requires a contractual monthly amount (currently VND 7-8 million); choosing `part_time` requires an hourly amount (currently VND 26,000/hour). Store the wage basis and amount as effective-dated compensation terms. A later raise or rate change creates a new term and never mutates a finalized or historical pay period.
- Compensation interpretation: all entered monthly salaries and hourly rates are gross contractual amounts before employee-side insurance and PIT deductions. The payslip separately shows gross earnings, every deduction and its explanation, and resulting net pay. Net-salary gross-up and mixed gross/net contracts are excluded from MVP.
- Independent validation: the accountant validates the payroll cases, while one external specialist competent in Vietnamese labour law and payroll tax independently reviews and signs the exact rule matrix, cited source versions, and golden cases once before real payroll use. The specialist's identity can be appointed later, but production remains blocked until the signed evidence exists.
- Approved MVP runtime architecture: Windows-native modular monolith using Next.js/TypeScript and PostgreSQL, with no Docker dependency for the MVP. Keep the deterministic payroll domain isolated from framework/database/delivery adapters, use a PostgreSQL-backed outbox/worker, store protected local document files with database metadata and hashes, expose only the isolated attendance ingress through Cloudflare Tunnel, and keep Docker/cloud storage as later optional adapters rather than release dependencies.
- Approved design section 1 — system boundary and data flow: the home Windows host is the sole authoritative system; the database, protected document store, payroll administration, and backup surface remain private; Cloudflare exposes only the isolated attendance ingress; raw clock events flow through accountant exception drafting and owner approval into an immutable attendance-period snapshot; payroll consumes only that snapshot plus effective-dated compensation and legal rules; finalization precedes PDF generation/delivery; Gmail is outbound-only and personal Zalo remains manual.
- Approved design section 2 — data model and immutable history: organization/people, contracts and effective-dated compensation, schedules and attendance, legal rule packs, pay runs and calculation lines, payslips/delivery, and audit are separate data groups; effective periods cannot overlap; VND uses integers after named exact-decimal rounding boundaries; raw attendance and finalized payroll are append-only; an approved attendance snapshot is hash-bound to payroll; corrections use linked adjustment records/runs; timestamps are stored in UTC and interpreted in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`; inactive employees and historical payroll records are retained rather than deleted.
- Approved design section 3 — scheduling and attendance: effective-dated opening hours and employee schedules are validation context rather than proof of work; paid breaks require no punches and cause no automatic deduction; the registered iPOS kiosk accepts employee code plus hashed six-digit PIN and explicit clock-in/out; success is shown only after durable server acceptance; raw timestamps are retained without automatic rounding; missing, duplicate, overlapping, out-of-shift, public-holiday, and 22:00-06:00 segments are flagged; the accountant drafts each classification and the owner approves it; full-time attendance variance does not automatically reduce monthly salary; part-time payroll consumes only approved payable duration; unresolved exceptions block the immutable monthly attendance snapshot.
- Approved design section 4 — deterministic payroll and legal rules: the pure payroll domain has no network/database/delivery dependency and returns the same trace for the same inputs/rule version; full-time ordinary pay starts from the effective monthly salary without automatic attendance deductions, while part-time ordinary pay uses approved duration and the effective hourly rate; regional floors are effective-dated; each insurance fund is independently eligible, based, capped, rated, and traced, with part-time eligibility re-evaluated monthly; the resident 12-month-contract PIT lane uses the effective progressive schedule and deductions, excludes the Resolution 43/2026/QH16 business-income reduction, and excludes annual finalization; holiday entitlement and the daytime holiday overtime addition are separate components rather than a universal 400% rule; night work and every other unsupported case fail closed; every result line records formula, inputs, source, rule version, and rounding.
- Approved design section 5 — payslip, delivery, and security: the Vietnamese A4 PDF itemizes wage basis, approved hours, earnings, employee deductions, net pay, employer contributions, rule version, and payslip ID while excluding unnecessary identifiers; each employee PDF uses a strong random password delivered separately and stored only under Windows-backed protection; every email draft is hash-bound to one finalized payslip, employee, verified address, and pay period, requires owner preview/re-authentication, uses one recipient with no CC/BCC, and records provider acceptance separately from delivery; Gmail uses minimum-scope OAuth 2.0 and a protected refresh token after 2-Step Verification and real-account release tests; personal Zalo is manual-only and future OA delivery uses an adapter; accountant and owner have separate Argon2id-plus-TOTP accounts with backend-enforced maker-checker permissions, while the iPOS kiosk has attendance-only authority; retention and deletion are record-class based.
- Approved design section 6 — Windows operations, backup, verification, and release gates: use loopback-only PostgreSQL and private administration services, a separately isolated attendance ingress behind `cloudflared`, and a no-port worker; each service has explicit ownership, health, startup, recovery, and shutdown checks. Temporary quick tunnels are pilot-only; real use requires a named tunnel and owner-controlled domain. Backups write only to `G:\PaySlip-Backups`, use independent authenticated encryption and a separately held recovery secret, keep 14 daily/8 weekly/12 monthly restore points, alert on failure/capacity/freshness, and prove restore into a temporary location. Release is blocked until the legal rule pack and golden cases are signed, attendance exceptions are resolved, required tests pass, Gmail/iPOS/tunnel/backup behavior is directly proven, two parallel payroll cycles reconcile, and the incident runbook exists.
- Approved product-experience requirement: the administration and kiosk experiences must be easy to learn, low-step, Vietnamese-first, and visually premium without hiding calculation or approval detail. This is a release requirement, not optional polish. “Simple” means progressive disclosure, one clear primary action, plain-language errors with a next step, and bounded task flows; “premium” means a coherent design system, strong typography and spacing, accessible interaction states, responsive layouts, and visual-regression review rather than ornamental effects.
- Approved execution requirement: the canonical design and implementation plan must be executable either directly by Codex or as bounded Hermes work packets. Every packet has stable IDs, prerequisites, exact owned files, exclusions, allowed actions, approval gates, required artifacts, must-pass verification, stop conditions, and a structured handoff. Codex remains the integrator/reviewer and independently checks critical evidence before accepting a packet; an agent cannot widen scope or convert static/dry-run evidence into runtime or production proof.
